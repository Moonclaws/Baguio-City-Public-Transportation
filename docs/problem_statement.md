# Problem Statement — Baguio City Public Transportation

## The Problem
Our project aims to help tourists and local residents who have difficulty
commuting via jeepney because they are unfamiliar with jeepney routes and
staging areas leading to their desired destinations. As a result, many choose
more expensive transportation options, such as taxis.

## The System
The system will use jeepney routes, staging areas, passenger demand, and
destination information to recommend the correct jeepney routes and predict
passenger demand.

## Success Measures
- Accuracy of route recommendations.
- Accuracy of passenger demand predictions.
- Reduction in commuters' travel time and transportation costs.
- Number of users successfully reaching their destinations by jeepney.

## Data Needed
- Jeepney routes and destinations
- Jeepney staging areas and terminals
- Passenger counts
- Popular tourist destinations
- Jeepney schedules and estimated waiting times
- Commuter travel preferences
- Common pickup and drop-off locations
